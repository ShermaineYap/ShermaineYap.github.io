Final Year Project· With SAS·Jan – Sep 2026

Predicting injury risk and performance in badminton

Badminton analytics is split in two. Performance is studied by one group and injuries by another, so an athlete can look excellent on every performance metric while quietly accumulating risk that nobody is tracking.

I closed that gap with three models behind one dashboard: match outcomes forecast from Glicko-2 ratings and an order-symmetric logistic regression; high-risk training sessions flagged by a calibrated XGBoost model with SHAP explaining every prediction; and strokes detected straight off broadcast rally footage. The dataset didn't exist, so I wrote the scraper and released it.

Real Time Systems· Individual build·May 2026

Two ways to read a firehose, measured against each other

Wikipedia publishes every edit made anywhere on the site as a live event stream — 5 to 30 events a second, spiking past 100. I built two architecturally different pipelines to consume it in Rust, one async on Tokio and one on OS threads, and benchmarked them head to head with Criterion under identical live load.

The async pipeline held a p99 of 74 µs against the threaded one's 2,699 µs, comfortably inside a 2 ms soft deadline. Zero-copy JSON parsing was proven independently by a custom tracking allocator measuring the exact heap delta on the parse hot path — not asserted, measured.
